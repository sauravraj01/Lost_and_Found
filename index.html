Everything you need to recover campus items.

FindIT is the lost-and-found board for JIIT. Sign in with your campus email, post an item, and let the board do the rest — including a mail if something looks like a match.

Quick reporting

Submit a lost or found report with a name, description, and an optional photo in one form.

Campus board

Search, filter, and sort every post so you can find the item without scrolling endlessly.

My reports

Open your own posts from the profile menu and keep track of possible matches.

Match email

If a found report looks like your lost item, a mail can be sent to your JIIT inbox.

  • Report lost or found items with a photo and a short description.
  • Search and filter the campus board by lost, found, matches, or your posts.
  • Track only your reports from the profile menu when you need them.
  • Get an email on your JIIT inbox if a found item looks like a match.
